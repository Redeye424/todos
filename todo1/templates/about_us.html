THE IDEA BEHIND KNOX

Why I Made Knox

I didn't make Knox because people don't know how to make a todo list.

I made it because sometimes making the todo list is the easy part.

Actually doing the things on it? That's where things get difficult.

01

The Problem

There are a million todo apps out there. They let you write down what you need to do, organize it, give it a color, set a deadline, and then...

They leave you alone.

But what if the problem isn't that you forgot what you needed to do?

What if you know exactly what you need to do, but you just don't have the motivation to actually get up and do it?

02

That's Where Knox Comes In

Knox is designed around a simple idea:

"What if your todo list could argue with you?"

Instead of just showing you a list of tasks, Knox gives you an AI companion whose job is to convince you to actually get those tasks done.

You can make your todos, talk to Knox, explain why you don't want to do something, make excuses, or even try to change your priorities.

But Knox isn't just going to automatically agree with you.

03

Think You Have Something Better To Do?

Then prove it.

If you think you have something more important to do instead of the todo Knox is trying to get you to complete, you have to convince Knox that your new plan is actually worth doing.

This turns the normal productivity experience around.

Instead of you convincing yourself to procrastinate, you're trying to convince an AI that your excuse is actually a good reason.

And Knox is allowed to disagree.

04

Knox Has A Memory

Knox doesn't just look at one message at a time. Your conversations with Knox are saved so the AI can use the conversation history when talking with you.

Knox can also see your current todos and your karma, which means the AI has more context than just "Hey, go do your homework."

Your todos, your conversations, your decisions, and your behavior all become part of the experience.

05

The Karma System

Knox also has a karma system.

Completing todos can increase your karma, while ignoring things and disappearing can make it go down.

Your karma can also affect how Knox treats you.

High Karma Knox trusts you more.
50 Karma You're doing alright.
Low Karma Knox is probably not impressed.

The goal isn't to make productivity feel like punishment. It's to make actually getting things done feel more interactive.

06

And Yes... Knox Runs On My Laptop

One of the things I wanted to do with this project was actually run my own AI instead of just sending everything to some massive cloud service.

Knox uses Ollama to run the AI model on my own laptop.

Yes. My AI is literally running on a laptop.

And honestly, that makes it pretty cool.

I can control the model, control how Knox behaves, give it information about the user's todos, give it the conversation history, and build the personality specifically around this website.

It isn't just some generic chatbot that happens to have a todo list next to it.

Knox was built specifically to be Knox.

Also, if Knox happens to be offline sometimes... please understand that the "server" is an old laptop doing its absolute best. 😭

07

A Little More Eco-Friendly 🌱

Another reason I wanted to build Knox the way I did was to make it more eco-friendly.

Instead of sending every conversation to a ai data center where every message cost a cup of water and doubles the energy bill, Knox's AI runs locally on my own laptop using Ollama.

That means my little AI lives right here with me.

I'm not going to pretend that running an AI on a laptop has zero environmental impact. It still uses electricity, and computers still require resources to manufacture.

But for this project, running the AI locally means I can experiment with Knox without needing to rely on a separate AI service for every single message, plus one less laptop in the trash.

I wanted Knox to be useful, personal, and a little different from the usual approach to AI.

Small AI. Local laptop. Hopefully a slightly smaller footprint. 🌱

Plus, I get to keep an old laptop useful instead of letting it sit around doing absolutely nothing.

07

Why Not Just Use A Normal AI?

You could.

There are plenty of extremely powerful AI systems available. But the point of Knox isn't simply to have the smartest possible chatbot.

The point is to have an AI that has a specific job:

Get you to do the thing you said you were going to do.

Because of that, I can build Knox around a specific personality and a specific purpose instead of trying to make it do everything.

And because my model is running locally, I'm also able to experiment with the system and change how Knox works without having to build the entire idea around a third-party AI service.

08

The Actual Goal

At the end of the day, Knox isn't supposed to magically make someone productive.

I made it because sometimes you don't need another productivity technique.

You don't need another colorful checklist.

You don't need another notification telling you that something is overdue.

Sometimes you just need someone — or something — to tell you to stop making excuses and go do it.

That's what I wanted Knox to be, a combination of duolingo and google todos.

Your todo list has a voice now.

Good luck arguing with it. 😈

Meet Knox.

Make your todos. Talk to your AI. Get things done.
